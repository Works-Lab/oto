# OTO v2 Overview

## What It Is

OTO v2 is a compact whiteboard-cleaning hardware project and FDM mechanism testbed.

It builds on [OTO](https://github.com/Keem-tt/OTO-Whiteboard-Cleaner) with a simpler public direction: faster, cheaper, and sleeker.

The project is being used to turn a clean concept into physical iterations that can be printed, assembled, serviced, observed, and changed.

## What It Is Testing

OTO v2 is focused on practical mechanical questions:

- how printed rails and chassis geometry behave in real motion
- how pad interfaces should attach, wear, and be replaced
- how fastener bosses hold up across assembly and service cycles
- where printed parts are enough and where off-the-shelf parts make more sense
- how FDM constraints shape the design instead of being treated as an afterthought

## What Is Intentionally Not Solved Yet

The project is not presenting a finished product, final mechanism, final material set, final BOM, or validated durability target yet.

Public documentation should stay close to what has been built, tested, and observed. Open questions belong in `docs/design-questions.md` until there is evidence from physical iterations.
